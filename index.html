Fifa World Cup 2026

A fan guide to the biggest soccer tournament coming to North America.

About the Tournament

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is one of the biggest soccer events in the world. The tournament is hosted by Canada, Mexico, and the United States. It is also the first men's World Cup to include 48 teams, which gives more countries a chance to compete on the world stage.

Why the 2026 World Cup Is Special

This tournament is historic because it is being shared by three host countries. Fans from around the world will travel across North America to watch matches, support their national teams, and experience different cities. With 104 matches scheduled, the 2026 World Cup will be larger than any previous men's World Cup.
